Who We Are

Safe, Reliable, and Smart Electrical Engineering

ONE8 ELECTRICAL SOLUTIONS is a full-service electrical engineering firm specializing in planning, building, and servicing LT/HT distributions, industrial automations, and residential safety networks.

Founded by Hanuman Yadav, our business has established a reputation for top-tier work and transparency. We strictly adhere to corporate safety specifications defined by Indian Standards (IS) and local grid regulations, offering custom panel boards, cabling operations, energy conservation consulting, and solar grids installation.

Our Methodology

Standard Operating Procedure

How we maintain highest safety standards and quality across residential and industrial projects.

Step 01

Site Inspection

Thorough inspection of site parameters, power utility specifications, and cabling layout requirements.

Step 02

Detailed Planning

Design wiring schematics, calculate safety parameters, select equipment, and provide detailed estimations.

Step 03

Installation Phase

Execution of physical wiring, panel mounting, and power cable termination under strict oversight.

Step 04

Rigorous Testing

Earth resistance checks, load tests, dielectric insulation audits, and circuit breakers simulations.

Step 05

Handover & Support

System commissioning, sharing of layout diagrams, safety orientation, and post-installation reviews.
