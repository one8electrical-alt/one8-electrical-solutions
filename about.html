Owner Profile & Story

Led by Hanuman Yadav

At ONE8, engineering begins with complete safety and ends with absolute trust.

Founded and managed by Hanuman Yadav, an electrical solutions specialist with over 10 years of onsite expertise, ONE8 ELECTRICAL SOLUTIONS was established with the vision of bridging the gap between local services and corporate electrical requirements in Tonk and Jaipur districts. Under his management, we have served manufacturing plants, high-rise buildings, institutions, and houses, designing custom electrical switchboards, executing cable joints, and configuring rooftop solar grids. We believe in providing robust setups backed by valid guarantees and certified quality tests.

We work exclusively with certified copper conductors, ISO panel cabinetry, and safety switchgears from world leaders like Schneider, Siemens, Legrand, and L&T Electrical.

Our Mission

To deliver safe, reliable, and energy-efficient electrical contracting and panel fabrications with complete adherence to Indian Standards and client layouts.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the premier corporate and residential electrical solutions provider in Rajasthan, expanding our reach in automation and sustainable energy solar grids.

Core Values

Safety First compliance, zero-compromise on hardware specs, upfront pricing transparency, prompt emergency callout assistance, and lifelong relationship trust.
